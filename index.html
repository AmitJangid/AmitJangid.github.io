Research Profile

I specialize in the application of mathematical and computational modeling to solve complex biological problems. My work employs stochastic simulations, bifurcation analysis, large-scale data analysis, and network theory.

My current research investigates population genetics and the evolution of small protein regulatory networks in plant self-incompatibility systems. I also have extensive experience modeling regulatory networks in disease contexts, cell fate decisions, and the dynamics of NF-KB oscillators.
